Privacy Policy

Universal Smart TV Remote

Last updated: 1 November 2026
Developer: Ryan Wen
Android package: com.ryanw.universaltvcontroller
Contact: jspaceauto@gmail.com

This app controls compatible Android TV / Google TV devices over your local network. You do not need an app account. The free edition supports banner advertising and the Graphite theme. An optional, one-time Pro purchase removes advertising and unlocks all available themes, additional saved TVs and the sleep timer.

Advertising-enabled edition, 1 November 2026: this policy covers the update whose source now includes Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). The advertising-enabled build has been prepared for testing; device advertising/privacy testing is still pending. Earlier ads-free test builds are separate artifacts. This update is not yet publicly released or approved by Google. Advertising depends on the eligibility and privacy choices below, region and available ads. Optional automatic usage statistics and crash reporting remain separate, off by default and available only to adults who explicitly enable them.

1. Local remote control and saved settings

The app discovers TVs on the local network and sends commands to the TV you select. Pairing requires the code shown on that TV. Paired remote connections use TLS. Text you submit, YouTube searches and app-launch links are sent to the selected TV as part of the action you request. The TV and any destination app or service handle that content under their own policies.

The app stores settings on your phone, including saved TV names and network addresses, pairing certificates and trust information, shortcut preferences, appearance settings, Pro entitlement, separate advertising age and privacy choices, diagnostic consent choices and active timer information. These records support local operation; the app does not provide a developer-hosted account or cloud synchronization service.

Android backup: this version allows Android backup. Depending on Android version, manufacturer and your backup or device-transfer settings, local app preferences, saved TV information, credential files and other app data may be copied to your platform backup or another device. These platform-managed copies are separate from our optional reporting and are controlled through your device and backup provider. Clearing this app does not necessarily remove earlier backups. See Android's backup information.

2. Optional automatic usage and crash reporting

Optional automatic reporting is available only to users aged 18 or older. When you choose to enable reporting, the app first asks for an age range. Adult eligibility and an explicit reporting opt-in are both required. Reporting stays off for anyone under 18, anyone who does not answer, or anyone who declines. We use Sentry for JavaScript and native Android errors and crash reports, and Aptabase for usage events. Their cloud services receive enabled reports to help us understand feature use, diagnose problems and improve the app.

When enabled, reports may include:

  • App interactions: screens and features opened, scan counts, connection and pairing result categories, and whether actions such as text entry, YouTube search or shortcut creation occurred.
  • Purchase status: whether Pro was unlocked or a previous Pro purchase was found during restoration.
  • Crash and diagnostic information: error messages, stack traces, app and SDK versions, operating-system and device information, and related technical context.
  • Pseudonymous identifiers: Sentry's native Android SDK uses a random identifier retained for an app installation. Aptabase uses a session identifier generated in memory that changes on reload or after a period without events. On its servers, Aptabase also derives a daily pseudonymous user identifier from your connection IP and User-Agent using a per-app keyed hash. Its hash key rotates every 24 hours and is permanently deleted within 48 hours of creation. These identifiers can associate events within an installation, session or day.
  • Connection IP and approximate location: receiving services process the IP used to connect to them. Aptabase derives country and region from the IP and states that the raw IP and User-Agent are processed only in memory and are not stored. Sentry may derive approximate location from its receiving connection. The app does not request Android precise-location permission; this does not prevent IP-based approximate location processing.

Our custom usage events use a restricted list of properties and do not include the text of TV typing, YouTube search queries, pairing codes or custom shortcut names and links. JavaScript error reports apply additional redaction of some addresses and query content. Those JavaScript hooks do not cover every native crash report, and we do not promise that all native diagnostic context is removed.

Reporting is used for app analysis and reliability, not advertising. We do not use these reports to create an app login or ask you to provide your name, email or phone number to enable reporting. Sentry and Aptabase process the reporting data as our service providers; their applicable privacy information is available at Sentry Privacy and Aptabase Privacy.

3. Your automatic-reporting choice

You can use the remote without enabling reports. You can turn reporting off in Settings → Privacy. This stops new collection through the app's reporting controls; reports already queued or in transit may still finish sending. Turning reporting off does not delete reports already received.

If you previously enabled reporting under an earlier privacy notice, reporting remains off until you explicitly choose to enable it after reading the updated notice. A previous choice to keep reporting off remains in effect.

4. Manual problem reports and support email

Report a problem in Settings is available to users of all ages. It does not require an age check, Pro purchase, app account or consent to automatic reporting. Please use respectful language and do not include passwords, pairing codes or personal information. This is a reminder, not an automatic language filter.

The description stays in the form's memory until you close it; the app does not save it to its settings or send it to our reporting providers. The prepared report contains only the description you enter and the app version. Logs, TV names, network addresses, pairing information and other device identifiers are not attached automatically.

You can open an email draft addressed to jspaceauto@gmail.com, then review and send it in your email app. You can also copy the report and email it yourself. Preparing a draft or copying text does not send a report. Your email app and provider handle the draft, sender address and delivery under their own policies.

If you send us an email, we receive your sender address, message and any other information you choose to include. We handle this correspondence privately to respond to your request, investigate the problem and fix the app. It is not used for marketing or published as user content. Support correspondence may be retained while handling your request and where needed for legal obligations. You may contact us to request review or deletion of identifiable support correspondence; we do not promise automatic deletion or a fixed completion time.

5. Google Play purchases

Core remote controls are free, with the Graphite theme and one saved TV. Pro is an optional, one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase, not a subscription. It removes advertising and unlocks all available themes, additional saved TVs and the sleep timer. Google Play displays the applicable local price before you confirm a purchase.

The app checks purchase ownership to unlock, restore or reconcile Pro features. This can involve product identifiers, purchase state, purchase tokens and acknowledgment status. The configured regional price targets are not a promise of one worldwide price or of current purchase availability; Google Play must provide the actual local price before confirmation. Google Play handles payment, billing and transaction records under Google's privacy policy. The app does not receive your payment-card details or operate its own payment-card checkout. If you enable automatic reporting, Pro purchase and restoration status may also appear in the usage events described above.

6. Advertising and advertising privacy choices

Advertising-enabled builds integrate Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK 24.9.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. The free edition supports one fixed bottom banner, with no full-screen, launch or rewarded ads and no mediation. Pro removes the banner and new advertising requests. The app enables advertisements only after a successful current-session Google Play check confirms the free tier; a pending or unavailable check suppresses advertisements and preserves cached paid rights. Advertisements are suppressed while the app is in the background or an app overlay is open.

Separate advertising age choice: before the first advertising request, a neutral screen offers under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or skip/prefer not to say. We store only the coarse category locally, not a date of birth. Skipping or unknown age means no automatic UMP information request, no advertising SDK initialization and no advertising request; the remote remains usable. You can change this advertising choice in Settings. It is separate from adult eligibility for optional diagnostics and does not enable reports or send the chosen category to Sentry or Aptabase.

Protective settings for every served age range: all advertising requests, including adults, use child-directed treatment (TFCD), the advertising under-age-of-consent tag (TFUA), maximum ad content rating G and nonpersonalized requests. We do not relax those ad settings for adults. UMP uses its separate under-age flag for children and teens and not for adults; this UMP signal does not replace the advertising TFUA setting. Age-treatment and consent signals can be supplied to Google. These controls are not a claim of legal or Google Families certification.

Where applicable, UMP supplies the advertising consent flow and whether an advertisement may be requested. Settings provides the advertising privacy-options entry when Google requires it. If a required privacy form was already available, you may explicitly reopen that existing UMP form to change or withdraw choices after selecting Skip or obtaining Pro. This uses retained privacy state rather than an automatic UMP information refresh for unknown age; completing the form does not authorize advertisements for unknown age. If eligibility or privacy authorization cannot be resolved, advertising can remain unavailable while core remote controls and manual support remain usable. Nonpersonalized advertisements may still involve data processing and device storage; turning optional diagnostics off does not disable advertising processing for an advertisement you receive.

Google advertising connections can involve IP-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic/performance data and device or other identifiers for ad delivery, analytics and fraud prevention. This build is configured to remove Android AD_ID and Privacy Sandbox AdServices permissions, alongside the restrictive ad settings above. That does not establish that advertising is identifier-free or that IP and other advertising data are not processed. We do not request precise GPS location for advertisements. Google’s current SDK data-disclosure page describes version 25.5.0; it is not version-specific proof of every field transmitted by this app’s pinned 24.9.0 configuration. Final release checks must verify that configuration and its actual behavior.

Google’s collection and sharing for advertising are disclosed separately from the service-provider arrangements for optional Sentry and Aptabase reports. We do not sell diagnostic reports or manual support messages to advertisers. Google processes advertising data under its applicable policies and retention arrangements; choosing Pro or changing an advertising choice does not automatically delete records already received by Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps and Google’s SDK data disclosure.

7. Security, retention and deletion

Configured Sentry and Aptabase reporting connections use HTTPS. The app uses local storage for its own settings and TLS for paired TV remote connections. Security also depends on your phone, TV, network and platform backup settings; no method provides an absolute security guarantee.

Aptabase states that app analytics data may be stored for up to five years. Sentry reports follow the retention settings and service arrangements that apply to that service, together with applicable legal obligations. Turning reporting off does not erase reports already received. Local settings remain until removed through app features, Android's app-storage controls or uninstallation, subject to platform backup and restoration.

For a privacy question or deletion request, email jspaceauto@gmail.com. We can review requests, but there is no in-app account-deletion feature or self-service report-deletion API. Because reports use pseudonymous identifiers rather than an app account, we may not be able to locate an installation's reports from an email address alone. We do not promise deletion of records we cannot identify or records retained by Google or another provider under its own obligations. Google Play transaction records and platform backups are managed through their respective services.

Manual support email is handled as described in section 4. A support-email deletion request is separate from finding or deleting pseudonymous automatic reports.

8. Children and external services

The remote-control functions are available to users of all ages, subject to applicable store ratings and device or parental controls. Children and users whose adult eligibility is not confirmed cannot enable our optional automatic diagnostics or usage reporting. Choosing not to enable automatic reporting does not restrict the remote controls or manual support email. The reporting-eligibility choice is saved locally; we do not request a date of birth or send that selection to Sentry or Aptabase. Advertising choices and protective signals are handled separately as described in section 6. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact the address above so we can review it.

Email apps, privacy-policy links, app shortcuts, purchases and the TV's streaming apps may open or communicate with separate services. This policy describes our app's handling; those services have their own practices.

9. Changes and contact

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Material changes to optional reporting may require a new in-app choice before reporting starts. Questions: jspaceauto@gmail.com.
