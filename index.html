This app controls compatible Android TV / Google TV devices over your local network. No app account is required. Optional automatic usage and crash reporting is off by default and is available only to users aged 18 or older who separately choose to enable it.

The advertising-enabled free edition includes banner advertisements and the Graphite theme. An optional one-time Pro purchase removes advertisements and unlocks additional features. Advertising and automatic reporting have separate privacy controls. Declining automatic reporting does not prevent advertising-related processing in an advertising-enabled edition.

Advertising-enabled edition, 1 November 2026: this policy covers the update whose source now includes Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). The advertising-enabled build has been prepared for testing; device advertising/privacy testing is still pending. Earlier ads-free test builds are separate artifacts. This update is not yet publicly released or approved by Google. Advertising depends on the eligibility and privacy choices below, region and available ads. Optional automatic usage statistics and crash reporting remain separate, off by default and available only to adults who explicitly enable them.

1. Local remote control, stored data and permissions

The app discovers TVs on your local network and sends commands to the TV you select. Pairing requires the code displayed on that TV. Text you submit, YouTube searches and app-launch links are sent to that TV for the action you request. The TV and destination apps or services may then process that content under their own policies. This transfer to your TV is separate from automatic reporting to our providers.

The app stores TV names, network addresses and, when available, TV MAC addresses; pairing certificates and private keys; certificate fingerprints and trust records; saved-TV and forgotten-TV records; shortcuts; appearance preferences; Pro entitlement; privacy choices; and timer information. These support discovery, reconnection, pairing, wake commands and your chosen features. We do not provide a developer-hosted account or cloud synchronization service.

Network and Wi-Fi-state access support discovery and connection, and multicast access supports local discovery. The sleep timer uses a foreground service and may use notifications, wake locks and scheduled alarms to carry out a timer you set. The app's own features do not request precise GPS location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Location inferred from an internet connection is described separately below.

Paired remote-control connections use TLS. Discovery traffic and Wake-on-LAN packets do not have the same TLS protection and may be visible to other participants on your local network. Use a network and TV you trust.

Android backup: Android backup is enabled. Depending on Android version, manufacturer and your backup or device-transfer settings, preferences, saved TV information, credential files and other app data may be copied to a platform backup or another device. Platform-managed copies are separate from our reporting and are controlled through your device and backup provider. Clearing the app does not necessarily remove older backups. See Android backup information.

Removing a TV from the saved list does not necessarily erase its pairing credentials or revoke authorization held by the TV. Android's app-storage controls can clear this app's local data. Manage backups separately and, if necessary, remove the remote's authorization through the TV's controls.

2. Optional automatic usage and crash reporting

When you choose to enable reporting, the app asks for an age range. Adult eligibility and a separate affirmative reporting choice are both required. Reporting stays off for anyone under 18, anyone who does not answer, or anyone who declines. We use Sentry for JavaScript and native Android errors and crash reports, and Aptabase for usage events, to understand feature use and improve reliability.

Enabled reports may include:

  • App interactions: screens and features used, built-in app shortcuts launched, scan counts, connection and pairing result categories, and whether text entry, a YouTube search or shortcut creation occurred.
  • Purchase status: whether Pro was unlocked or a previous Pro purchase was found during restoration.
  • Diagnostics: error messages, stack traces, timestamps, app and SDK versions, language or locale, operating-system and device information, and related technical context.
  • Pseudonymous identifiers: Sentry's native Android SDK uses a random installation identifier. Aptabase uses an in-memory session identifier that changes on reload or after inactivity, and derives a daily identifier on its server from connection IP and User-Agent using a per-app keyed hash. Aptabase states that its hash key rotates every 24 hours and is deleted within 48 hours of creation. These identifiers can associate events within an installation, session or day; pseudonymous data is not necessarily anonymous.
  • Connection IP and approximate location: receiving services process the connection IP. Aptabase derives country and region and states that raw IP and User-Agent are processed in memory without being stored. Sentry may retain the connection IP with received events and derive approximate location; our current project does not enable Sentry's IP-storage prevention setting. This does not mean every report contains a stored IP. The absence of Android location permission does not prevent IP-based location processing.

Our custom usage events use a restricted property list and do not include TV typing text, YouTube search queries, pairing codes, or custom shortcut names and links. JavaScript error handling removes some addresses and query content and drops some diagnostic fields. These hooks do not sanitize every native crash report. We cannot guarantee that all incidental content is excluded from native diagnostic context.

These reports are used for app analytics and reliability, not advertising. You do not need to provide a name, email address or phone number to enable them. Sentry and Aptabase process reporting data as our providers. See Sentry Privacy and Aptabase Privacy.

3. Changing your reporting choice

You can use the remote without enabling reporting and turn reporting off at any time in Settings → Privacy. This stops new collection through the app's reporting controls. Reports already queued or in transit may still finish sending. Turning reporting off does not delete previously received reports.

An earlier reporting opt-in is not reused unless the locally saved adult eligibility and current notice requirements are met. Earlier opt-outs remain off. Where a material change requires renewed consent, reporting remains off until you make the new choice. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

4. Manual problem reports and support email

Report a problem is available to users of all ages and is separate from automatic reporting. It does not require an age check, Pro purchase, app account or consent to automatic reporting. It is not a public posting or chat service. Please avoid passwords, pairing codes and unnecessary personal information. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support for them.

Your description stays in form memory until the form closes. The app does not persist the draft in its settings or send it to Sentry or Aptabase. The prepared text contains the app name, app version and your description. No logs, TV names, network addresses, pairing data or other device identifiers are automatically attached.

You may open an email draft addressed to jspaceauto@gmail.com, review it and send it through your email app, or copy the text and send it yourself. Preparing a draft or copying text does not send a report to us. Your email app, clipboard service and email provider handle the content you transfer to them under their own practices, which may include draft or clipboard synchronization.

If you send an email, we receive the sender address, message and any attachments or information you choose to include. We use them privately to answer, investigate and resolve the request. We use Gmail to receive and manage support correspondence; see Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use support messages for marketing or publish them as user content.

Support retention: ordinary support correspondence is retained while the request is open and for up to 12 months after closure, then deleted from the support mailbox, unless applicable law requires a shorter period or a specific legal obligation or dispute requires longer retention. Provider-controlled backup or recovery copies may remain for the provider's applicable periods. You can request earlier deletion, subject to lawful exceptions.

If we learn that a child has sent personal information, we assess the applicable child-privacy requirements. When relying on a legally permitted one-time response, we promptly delete the child's contact information after responding, or immediately if we do not respond. The ordinary 12-month period does not override this rule. Ongoing support involving a child may require a parent or guardian and any notice or consent required by law.

5. Google Play purchases

Core remote controls are free, with the Graphite theme and one saved TV. Pro is an optional one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase, not a subscription. In the advertising edition it removes advertisements and unlocks all available themes, additional saved TVs and the sleep timer. Google Play displays the applicable local price before confirmation.

The app checks purchase ownership to unlock, restore or reconcile Pro. This involves product identifiers, purchase state, purchase tokens and acknowledgment status. Google Play handles payment, billing and transaction records under Google's Privacy Policy. The app does not receive payment-card details or provide its own card checkout. If you enable reporting, Pro purchase or restoration status may also appear in usage events. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not erase Google Play transaction records.

6. Advertising and advertising privacy choices

Advertising-enabled builds integrate Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK 24.9.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. The free edition supports one fixed bottom banner, with no full-screen, launch or rewarded ads and no mediation. Pro removes the banner and new advertising requests. The app enables advertisements only after a successful current-session Google Play check confirms the free tier; a pending or unavailable check suppresses advertisements and preserves cached paid rights. Advertisements are suppressed while the app is in the background or an app overlay is open.

Separate advertising age choice: before the first advertising request, a neutral screen offers under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or skip/prefer not to say. We store only the coarse category locally, not a date of birth. Skipping or unknown age means no automatic UMP information request, no advertising SDK initialization and no advertising request; the remote remains usable. You can change this advertising choice in Settings. It is separate from adult eligibility for optional diagnostics and does not enable reports or send the chosen category to Sentry or Aptabase.

Protective settings for every served age range: all advertising requests, including adults, use child-directed treatment (TFCD), the advertising under-age-of-consent tag (TFUA), maximum ad content rating G and nonpersonalized requests. We do not relax those ad settings for adults. UMP uses its separate under-age flag for children and teens and not for adults; this UMP signal does not replace the advertising TFUA setting. Age-treatment and consent signals can be supplied to Google. These controls are not a claim of legal or Google Families certification.

Where applicable, UMP supplies the advertising consent flow and whether an advertisement may be requested. Settings provides the advertising privacy-options entry when Google requires it. If a required privacy form was already available, you may explicitly reopen that existing UMP form to change or withdraw choices after selecting Skip or obtaining Pro. This uses retained privacy state rather than an automatic UMP information refresh for unknown age; completing the form does not authorize advertisements for unknown age. If eligibility or privacy authorization cannot be resolved, advertising can remain unavailable while core remote controls and manual support remain usable. Nonpersonalized advertisements may still involve data processing and device storage; turning optional diagnostics off does not disable advertising processing for an advertisement you receive.

Google advertising connections can involve IP-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic/performance data and device or other identifiers for ad delivery, analytics and fraud prevention. This build is configured to remove Android AD_ID and Privacy Sandbox AdServices permissions, alongside the restrictive ad settings above. That does not establish that advertising is identifier-free or that IP and other advertising data are not processed. We do not request precise GPS location for advertisements. Google’s current SDK data-disclosure page describes version 25.5.0; it is not version-specific proof of every field transmitted by this app’s pinned 24.9.0 configuration. Final release checks must verify that configuration and its actual behavior.

Google’s collection and sharing for advertising are disclosed separately from the service-provider arrangements for optional Sentry and Aptabase reports. We do not sell diagnostic reports or manual support messages to advertisers. Google processes advertising data under its applicable policies and retention arrangements; choosing Pro or changing an advertising choice does not automatically delete records already received by Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps and Google’s SDK data disclosure.

7. Security, retention and deletion

Sentry and Aptabase reporting connections use HTTPS. Paired TV remote-control connections use TLS; local discovery and wake traffic have the limits described in section 1. Local app data relies on app storage and device protections; this is not a claim that every stored preference has separate app-level encryption. Email transport and provider backups are controlled by the services involved. No method guarantees absolute security.

Aptabase states that app analytics data is stored for up to five years. Its daily hash-key deletion does not mean that analytics events are deleted after 48 hours. Our Sentry organization currently uses the Developer plan, for which Sentry states that error-event data is retained for 30 days. This period applies to event data, not every account, issue summary, audit or security record the provider maintains. We will reassess this policy if the applicable plan or retention changes. Google controls retention of Play transactions and advertising records under its applicable arrangements. Ordinary support retention is explained in section 4.

Local data remains until removed through the relevant app controls, Android app-storage controls or uninstallation, subject to backup and restoration. Removing a saved-TV entry is not a complete data reset. Device and backup copies must be managed separately.

For a privacy or deletion request, email jspaceauto@gmail.com. There is no developer-managed app account to delete. This does not remove your rights concerning personal information we process. We assess requests, take reasonable identity-verification steps when necessary, and respond within applicable legal periods. We will explain any lawful retention or inability to identify requested records.

A sender email address alone may not identify an installation's pseudonymous reports. Where reasonably necessary, we may ask for limited additional information to locate records, without requiring passwords or pairing secrets. We do not promise a per-installation deletion capability that the service does not provide. Requests about identifiable support correspondence can be handled separately from pseudonymous telemetry, Google Play records and platform backups.
